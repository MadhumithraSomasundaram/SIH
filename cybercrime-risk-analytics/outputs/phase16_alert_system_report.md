## 1. Objective

Phase 16 establishes an automated, production-grade **Alert & Notification Subsystem** built directly upon the validated foundations of previous phases:
- **Phase 9:** Calibrated continuous risk scores (0–100) and operational risk tiers (`LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL`).
- **Phase 11:** Frozen XGBoost inference pipeline and schema validation.
- **Phase 12:** Production FastAPI REST API.
- **Phase 13:** PostgreSQL/PostGIS spatial database models, indexing, and transactional CRUD.
- **Phase 14:** DBSCAN spatial clustering and analytical hotspot intelligence.
- **Phase 15:** GIS Risk Heatmap and analytical incident dashboard.

The overarching purpose is to convert static risk scores and spatial clusters into **actionable analytical alerts** whenever model-derived risk or geographic hotspot conditions satisfy documented thresholds.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Operational Boundary:**  
> All alerts generated by this framework represent **model-derived analytical signals** requiring authorized human review. The system:
> - Never retrains XGBoost or DBSCAN
> - Never automatically accuses any individual or citizen
> - Never freezes bank accounts or blocks transactions
> - Never contacts victims or targets without authorization
> - Never accesses unauthorized live banking, ATM, or NCRP systems
> - Never exposes sensitive credentials (passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVVs, full card numbers)

## 4. Alert Types

Documented in [`outputs/phase16_alert_type_definitions.csv`](file:///d:/SIH/SIH_2026/cybercrime_prediction/outputs/phase16_alert_type_definitions.csv):

1. **`CRITICAL_RISK_LOCATION`**  
   Triggered when an individual complaint prediction attains a calibrated risk score $\ge 80$. Indicates an urgent analytical likelihood of qualifying withdrawal activity within 24 hours.
2. **`HIGH_RISK_LOCATION`**  
   Triggered when a prediction risk score falls within $[60, 79]$. Directs proactive investigative monitoring.
3. **`HOTSPOT_CRITICAL_RISK`**  
   Triggered when a DBSCAN spatial cluster demonstrates an aggregate average risk score $\ge 80$ or contains at least one critical risk incident.
4. **`HOTSPOT_ELEVATED_RISK`**  
   Triggered when a DBSCAN spatial cluster demonstrates an average risk score $\ge 60$ or contains elevated high risk complaints.
5. **`WITHDRAWAL_HOTSPOT`**  
   Triggered when an analytical spatial cluster exhibits $\ge 15$ correlated historical cash withdrawal events.
6. **`ACTIVITY_SURGE`**  
   *Operational Status: UNAVAILABLE.* The current dataset does not provide sufficient longitudinal baseline history to establish non-misleading surge multipliers without fabricating synthetic baselines. Explicitly documented as unavailable per specification.

## 5. Thresholds & Calibration

Reusing the validated Phase 9 operational tiers:
- **`LOW` (0–39):** Routine analytical baseline; no operational notification generated.
- **`MODERATE` (40–59):** Secondary priority; filtered from primary immediate alert feed.
- **`HIGH` (60–79):** High priority alert; mandatory authorized human review.
- **`CRITICAL` (80–100):** Urgent priority alert; expedited authorized human review required.

All thresholds are centralized in [`config/alert_config.json`](file:///d:/SIH/SIH_2026/cybercrime_prediction/config/alert_config.json) rather than hardcoded throughout the codebase.

## 6. Deduplication & Cooldown

To prevent alert fatigue and redundant transmissions during recurring generation runs, the system implements a two-stage deduplication mechanism:

1. **Intra-Batch Collision Guard:**  
   Constructs a composite deduplication key:
   $$\text{Key} = \text{alert\_type} : \text{location\_or\_hotspot\_id} : \text{severity} : \text{time\_bucket (YYYYMMDD\_HH)}$$
   Duplicate alerts generated within the same hour bucket for the identical entity are suppressed.
2. **Configurable Cooldown Windows:**  
   - `CRITICAL`: 30-minute cooldown
   - `HIGH`: 60-minute cooldown
   - `MODERATE`: 120-minute cooldown  
   Existing persisted alerts within their active cooldown duration prevent redundant generation. In validation testing, 45 out of 51 duplicate candidate alerts were cleanly suppressed on an immediate rerun.

## 8. Alert Lifecycle & State Workflow

The system enforces strict state machine transitions:

```
                     ┌───────────────┐
                     │      NEW      │
                     └───────┬───────┘
                             │
              ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
              │                             │
              ▼                             ▼
     ┌─────────────────┐           ┌─────────────────┐
     │  ACKNOWLEDGED   │           │    DISMISSED    │
     └────────┬────────┘           └─────────────────┘
              │                             ▲
              ▼                             │
     ┌─────────────────┐                    │
     │    IN_REVIEW    ├────────────────────┘
     └────────┬────────┘
              │
              ▼
     ┌─────────────────┐
     │    RESOLVED     │
     └─────────────────┘
```

- Direct transitions from `NEW` to `RESOLVED` are disallowed.
- Transitioning `CRITICAL` alerts to `RESOLVED` by `SYSTEM` is forbidden: authorized human review is mandatory.
- Every state transition generates an immutable audit record in `alert_audit_log`.

## 10. Notification Architecture & Safety

Structured in package `notifications/`:
- `BaseNotifier`: Abstract adapter with automated payload sanitization.
- `LogNotifier`: Outputs structured operational summaries to standard application logs.
- `EmailNotifier`: Standard SMTP adapter reading connection parameters from environment variables (`SMTP_HOST`, `SMTP_PORT`, `ALERT_EMAIL_FROM`, `ALERT_EMAIL_TO`).
- `NotificationDispatcher`: Orchestrates multi-channel delivery.

### Safety Guarantee:
- **Default Mode:** `DRY RUN / LOG ONLY` (`ENABLE_EMAIL_ALERTS=false`).
- **Zero Real External Delivery by Default:** No live SMS, external push, or unauthorized external emails are transmitted.
- **Strict Data Scrubbing:** All payloads strictly omit account numbers, card numbers, CVVs, PINs, OTPs, passwords, and phone numbers.
- **Mandatory Disclaimer:** Embedded in every notification header and body:
  > *"This alert is a model-derived analytical signal and does not establish criminal activity or identify a criminal."*

## 14. Production Requirements

To transition this analytical prototype to a live law-enforcement operational center, the following prerequisites must be fulfilled:
1. **Operational Authorization:** Formal statutory approval under legal guidelines.
2. **Access Control:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) with OAuth2 / JWT authentication for analysts.
3. **Hardware Security:** Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) and encrypted PostgreSQL storage (TDE).
4. **False Positive Monitoring:** Periodic model calibration reviews to monitor geographic and temporal concept drift.
5. **Human-in-the-Loop Protocol:** Mandatory standard operating procedures requiring human corroboration before any administrative intervention.
