## Predictive Cybercrime Intelligence System
**Problem Statement ID:** 26184  
**Speaking Pace:** Confident, deliberate (approx. 130–140 words per minute)

### [00:00 – 00:12] PROBLEM
> *"Respected judges: When citizens fall victim to cyber financial fraud, criminals rapidly transfer stolen funds across digital mule accounts—but their final step is converting that money into physical cash at an ATM. Traditional policing is purely reactive, initiating investigations days after the cash is already gone."*

### [00:12 – 00:24] SOLUTION
> *"Our solution is a **Predictive Cybercrime Analytics Framework** that forecasts geographic areas where future cash withdrawal activity is statistically more likely within 24 hours of complaint registration, enabling proactive beat patrol allocation."*

### [00:24 – 00:36] TECHNOLOGY
> *"We combine an **optimized XGBoost classifier** handling severe class imbalance, **SHAP** for transparent feature explainability, **PostGIS** for sub-10ms spatial queries, **DBSCAN** to isolate 40 physical cashout hotspots, and a **FastAPI** backend powering a live GIS command dashboard."*

### [00:36 – 00:48] OPERATION
> *"Our pipeline operates seamlessly:  
> Incoming Complaint $\longrightarrow$ Probability $\longrightarrow$ Risk Score $\longrightarrow$ SHAP Drivers $\longrightarrow$ Hotspot Overlay $\longrightarrow$ Operational Alert $\longrightarrow$ Authorized Human Review in our secure case workspace."*

### [00:48 – 01:00] IMPACT & ETHICAL BOUNDARY
> *"By moving law enforcement from reactive forensics to explainable predictive intelligence, we empower officers to deter cashouts in priority corridors. Crucially, the system is strictly decision-support: it does not guarantee withdrawals or accuse individuals—it ensures every action has human accountability. Thank you!"*
